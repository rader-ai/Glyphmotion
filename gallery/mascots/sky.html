Mascot pitch for Meridian, the shared calendar that follows the sun

NOMI

A small shadow-keeper who tells time by how long they stand. Their pin catches the light. Their shadow keeps the schedule.

feeling: content

Try: move your pointer over the stage, it becomes the sun. Secret: grab the glowing pin and slide sideways to move the real sun.

Why this character

Every calendar has a sundial inside it

A cloud, a sun, a raindrop with eyes. Every calendar app would pick one of those. Meridian follows the sun, so we asked what the oldest calendar object on earth is. The answer is a sundial, and the heart of a sundial is one small upright pin.

The idea

The pin is called a gnomon. It stands in the light and throws a shadow, and the shadow tells the time. Nomi is that pin with a face: a warm pebble body, a glowing blade, two small arms, and a shadow that is half of the character.

What nobody else has

Nomi's shadow runs a beat late, stretches long at dawn and dusk, and shrinks to a puddle at noon. Now and then it moves on its own. Nomi turns around and gives it a suspicious look. A belly strip of day ticks glows at the current hour.

Why people will love it

You can pet it, feed it a spark, pick it up, and slide its pin to move the sun across the page. It sleeps when you leave, worries when a form fails, and never nags. It feels like a small pet that happens to know what time it is.

Character rules

How Meridian uses Nomi

  1. Appears at moments, not everywhere.Welcome, empty states, errors, long waits, finished work, and the footer. Never in the middle of a form or on a billing page.
  2. Never blocks anything.Nomi lives beside content, never over it. One tap hides or sleeps them, and the choice is remembered. Reduced motion gives calm still poses that still change with feeling.
  3. The shadow is always honest.It follows the real time of day and the real light. Never fake it, and never give Nomi a shadow that points toward the sun.
  4. Quiet voice.If Nomi speaks, it is a short plain sentence in lowercase with no exclamation marks, like "that day wandered off." No jokes at the user's expense, no guilt, no nagging.
  5. Bad news gets worry, never mockery.Errors make Nomi concerned and never silly. Celebration is for real wins, not every click.
  6. Wake kindly.Nomi falls asleep when the user is away or it is late, and never asks for attention. Anything the user touches wakes them gently.
