01 / Meet her

Vesper

A small bat who hangs from the signal wire, pings before she speaks, and always waits to hear that you got the message.

  • Gentle
  • A little shy
  • Nocturnal
  • Big ears
  • Obsessed with delivery

Her name is the evening hour, when bats wake up. She is made of the same characters as the rest of Carrier: indigo fur, amber eyes, cyan pings. Nothing about her is a robot, a screen glitch, or falling green code.

Try this. Move the pointer and watch her eyes and ears follow. Stop moving for a while and she falls asleep. Click an empty spot to toss a blip and she will swoop for it.

02 / Why a bat

She does what Carrier does

Carrier's promise is "Say it once. Know it arrived." A bat is the one animal that lives by exactly that. She sends a sound out, waits, and uses the answer to know where everything is.

Ping, then listen

Every action has two halves. Vesper sends a cyan ring. A moment later an amber ring comes back and lands in her ears, and they flare. That is delivery confirmation you can see, and it is the whole product in one gesture.

Nobody expects a bat

Messaging mascots are birds, envelopes, paper planes and robots. A bat is none of those. She hangs from the wire itself, so her home is the infrastructure. She is also the only mascot here that sleeps the right way up in a footer: upside down.

She is built from the language

Her ears are receiver dishes. They glow when she hears something. Her wings are scalloped membranes with a cyan rim light, her pings are the same rings as the buttons, and her wire is the same line as the nav. Nothing is pasted on.

She teaches the product states

People read a face faster than a banner. Concerned means something failed. Busy means a message is in flight. Proud means it landed. Asleep means all quiet. Ten feelings, each with a trigger the site already has.

Why people will share her

She falls asleep in the footer, wraps herself up when poked too often, and reveals a hidden tagline when you ping. Add a pointer that she actually watches and a secret in her ears, and you get clips people send to a friend.

The honest risk

Bats can read as spooky. So she has a round face, warm amber eyes, a small smile with two tiny fangs, and blushes when petted. She is cozy, not Halloween.

03 / Emotion sheet

Ten feelings, all live

Each card is a real Vesper running right now. Press a button and the big one on the left feels it too.

Content

Default. Nothing is wrong. Slow breathing, soft smile, wings wrapped.

Curious

The pointer moves near her, or a field gets focus. Ears up, head tilted.

Delighted

She is petted, catches a blip, or an echo comes back. Happy eyes, flutter, a lap of the stage.

Surprised

A poke or a sudden grab. Wings snap open, ears shoot up, she swings.

Sleepy

No one has moved for a while, or it is daytime. Heavy lids, drooping ears, a yawn.

Asleep

Idle for long, or sent to sleep. Wrapped up, little z's, slow breath. Any movement wakes her.

Shy

Poked three times quickly. She hides behind her wings, peeks out, blushes and trembles.

Concerned

A form error or a failed send. Worried brows, drooped ears, a small frown. Never blames.

Proud

The message was delivered. Chest out, wings wide, a gold glint on the membrane.

Focused

Something is loading or sending. She takes off with a packet and flies a steady loop.

06 / Character rules

How the brand uses her

  1. She lives where status lives.Nav corner, empty states, loading, success, footer. Never over a form, a price or a paragraph.
  2. She never blocks.One tap minimizes her, one tap sends her to sleep, and the choice is remembered. Nothing on the page waits for her.
  3. She reacts to the product, not to blame.Concerned means "something failed", never "you did it wrong". Error copy stays plain and kind.
  4. She speaks rarely.Pings and captions of six words or fewer, in plain lowercase sentences. No jokes at the visitor's expense, no emoji, no baby talk.
  5. She never appears in payment, legal or security screens.Those moments need calm and trust, not a character. She is also never a cursor replacement.
  6. Three colors only.Indigo fur, amber eyes and warm things, cyan for sound and signal. She always hangs from a wire and is always a little sleepy in daylight.
