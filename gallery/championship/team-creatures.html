Shoal, the workspace for teams that move together

Work moves better as a school.

Plan, decide and ship in one shared space. Shoal keeps everyone heading the same way, without a meeting to say so.

Your cursor is a lantern. Move slowly and the school comes to look. Move fast and it scatters. Tap anywhere on the water for a pulse. Secret: type whale .

01 / The mark

A wordmark made of fish.

Every letter is a few hundred fish holding formation. Hover to spook them, and watch them return. The same mark sits in the nav at 56 pixels tall.

nav size, 200 x 56

02 / Buttons

Buttons that have somewhere to be.

Each control is a small habitat. Hover, press, focus and hold all change what the creatures do. Press the first one a few times.

Primary

Coral and shelter

Hover: fish tuck in close and glow. Press: they burst apart and a ripple rolls out. Release: they drift home in a new color.

Secondary

The jellyfish

Hover: the bell pulses faster and leans toward you. Press: it jets away, then sinks back to its place.

Ghost

Plankton underline

Hover: the underline is a drift of plankton that follows your pointer. Press: it flares. Focus: it gathers and lights up.

Icon

Fireflies in sync

Hover: they drift toward you and start to flash together, as real fireflies do. Press: one shared flash.

Toggle

Let the school sleep

Switch it on and the knob fish swims across and dozes. Every school on this page dims and slows down with it.

Destructive

Release the school

Press and hold (or hold Space). The fish grow restless and the fill rises. At full they flee for good, then swim back after a moment.

03 / The reef

Hold the water.

Press and hold anywhere on the reef. Plankton and fish gather into a light that grows. Let go, or wait until it is full, and the whole reef blooms.

Touch and hold the water, or hold this button for the same thing.

04 / Language notes

Five rules of the world.

01

One lamp in ink water

Everything sits in near-black blue. Foam cyan is the living color, lamp amber is attention, coral is action. Nothing else gets a vote.

02

Density is depth

One ramp, always read the same way: sparse is far or faint, dense is near or lit. Creatures use arrow glyphs so you can read which way they face.

.:-=+*#@
> < ^ v / \
03

Behavior, not animation

Nothing here is a looping clip. Creatures flock, flee, follow light, feed, flash in sync and sleep. Motion comes from forces, so no two moments repeat.

04

Every touch has an echo

A press scatters, a hover draws near, a hold gathers. Whatever you do, the world answers and then drifts back home. Nothing ends in a snap.

05

Night is a state

Sleep dims color and slows every school, but text keeps its contrast. Reduced motion freezes the simulation and keeps every state change as a still frame.

Everyone sleeps somewhere.
