CH 01 / signal locked

Say it once. Know it arrived.

Carrier sends the message that matters to every phone, inbox and screen on your list, then shows you who got it.

Read the language

Hold Space or press and hold the hero to charge a broadcast. Move to tune. Click to ping.

01 / The mark

A wordmark that is a carrier wave, carrying itself.

The mark is born as a bare sine wave. The wave then modulates up into the letters, row by row from the center line, and each character flashes amber the moment it locks.

IdleA raster line drifts down the letters. A few cells flicker and recover.
AttentionHover or focus splits the color channels one cell apart and speeds the sweep.
FormCarrier wave, then modulation, then lock. About three seconds on load.
Navigation size. Hover it.

02 / Buttons

Six ways to press. One of them you will press again.

Every state is drawn in characters. Hover tunes, press injects energy, keyboard focus locks on with the same gesture as hover plus an amber outline.

Primary

Hover sweeps a dial cursor across and locks the fill. Press flashes it hot.

Secondary

A pulse circles the border. Hover sends two. Press pulls the frame inward.

Ghost

The underline is a flat line until you arrive, then it becomes a live wave.

Icon

Rings radiate while hovered. A press sends one big ring outward.

Toggle, destructive

Cuts the carrier for the whole page. The hero goes flat, the letters dissolve into static. Press again to restore it.

Transmit, the one you press again

Every press launches a packet with its own hex address, and each one rides a different channel color. Mash it.

03 / Menu

The menu is a spectrum analyzer.

Each nav item owns a band. Hover raises its bars and sends a packet down the wire from wherever you were last. The Platform dropdown opens with a raster retrace, then a tuner needle slides between items.

  • Hover a nav item: bars rise, a packet runs the wire.
  • Open Platform: raster line, noise clears, needle tracks you.
  • Arrow keys move through items. Escape closes.
  • On a phone the burger becomes an X and the whole screen retunes.

04 / Language notes

Five rules for anything that wants to be Carrier.

Short on purpose. If a new component breaks one of these, it is not Carrier yet.

RULE 1

Phosphor, not paint.

Everything lit decays over about half a second. Nothing blinks off. Moving things leave a trail.

RULE 2

Ten steps of signal.

One ramp for every intensity, from silence to full power. Letters live on the top four.

.:-=+*#%@
RULE 3

Carrier first.

Motion begins as a wave. Letters, bars and packets are what the wave carries, so they arrive riding it.

~-~-/\/\-~-~-
RULE 4

Decode, never fade in.

Text resolves from noise, left to right, and flashes amber once as each character locks.

%#x+ locked
RULE 5

Color is status.

Cyan is searching. White is hot. Amber is locked or confirmed. Magenta is lost. No other meanings.

scan hot lock lost
