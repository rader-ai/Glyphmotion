Meltwork / project tracking that settles

Make it while it is liquid.

Meltwork keeps half-formed work moving. Pour in rough ideas, let them settle into shape, then cast what is ready. Everything here, including this page, is made of matter.

Move to steer the stream. Click or tap the pool to surge it.

01 / The mark

Cast, then cooled.

The wordmark rains in as drops of molten metal and fills the letters. It cools to steel with a slow sheen. Point at it to melt it, and it drips into a puddle.

02 / Buttons

Every button is a material.

Hover stirs it. Press changes it. Each one has a keyboard state too: tab to it and it wakes, then Enter or Space acts on it.

Primary / Ember

A pool of molten metal. Stir it with the pointer. Press and hold to push it down, release to make it erupt. The longer the hold, the bigger the fountain.

Secondary / Ink

Ink wells up under the cursor and spreads. Press to drop a blot that floods the frame, then slowly drains out the bottom.

Ghost / Smoke

Text that gives off smoke when you hover. Press and the letters rise away, then settle back in.

Icon / Sand

Made of 200 grains. Press and it falls apart, piles up, then pours itself back into the next shape. Pointer pushes grains aside.

Toggle / Cast switch

Live preview

A blob of metal slides between two ends. On is liquid and glowing. Off is cold steel, with steam where it cooled.

Destructive / Acid

Hold for one second. Acid eats the button outward from your pointer. Let go early and it grows back. Demo only, nothing is deleted.

03 / Menus

Menus are poured open.

The Materials menu in the header fills from the top like a mold, with a glowing edge and drips running ahead of it. Links in the bar grow a bead of metal underneath when you hover or tab to them. On a phone the menu button crumbles into sand and re-forms as a close mark.

Escape closes it.

04 / The pour

Hold the crucible.

Press and hold the crucible for two seconds. The metal rises, the walls glow and the vessel starts to shake. Let go at full charge and the whole page floods with liquid metal, then cools.

How to trigger it: hold the crucible with a mouse or a finger, or focus it and hold Space. Let go early and it only spills.

05 / Language notes

Five rules.

1. One material per meaning

Molten metal commits, ink chooses, smoke dismisses, sand is small, acid destroys. Never mix them on one control.

. : - = + * # % @

2. Heat is the only color

Brightness means energy. Black, then red, orange, gold and white-hot, with cold steel as the resting state. Accents appear only on their own material.

## ## ## ##

3. Things form, they never appear

Every element rains, pours, condenses or settles into place. Nothing fades in. Leaving is draining, crumbling or rising away.

4. The pointer is a hand

It stirs, warms and pushes. It never draws a cursor trail for show. Touch gets the same effect on tap.

5. Labels stay solid

Text in a control is always the brightest, steadiest thing in it. The matter moves around and behind the words, and only the words move when asked.
