Glyphmotion

Interfaces drawn in living glyphs.

A design language and a small engine for web interfaces made of full-color text characters, driven by simulation. Move your pointer through the word.

The idea

Simulated, not drawn

Water, fire, flocks and weather run as physics underneath. The glyphs are how you see them.

The interface is the medium

A button is the pool of molten metal, not a box in front of a scene.

Behavior, not loops

Things settle, get bored, notice you and fall asleep.
