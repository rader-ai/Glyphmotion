# Glyphmotion principles

These rules come out of two rounds of competition between four teams and six judges (see [history/](history/)). They are what separated the strongest work from the rest.

## The medium

1. **Every pixel is a glyph.** Shape comes from which character you pick, not only its color. Use density ramps (` .:-=+*#%@`) for light and depth, and line glyphs (`- \ | /`) for direction: spray, rain, wind, fins.
2. **Light is the art.** A dark ground, a disciplined palette and a soft glow do more than detail. The judges rewarded pages lit by one source (one sun, one lantern, one heat) over pages lit everywhere at once.
3. **Forms must read.** A whale must look like a whale at a glance, not a cloud of `@`. Give characters a clear silhouette, an outline or rim light, and a face that survives being small. This was the most common failure in both rounds.

## The interface

4. **The control is the medium.** A button is a pool, a coral, a cloud, not a rectangle with a scene behind it. Keep the label as real HTML text so it stays readable and accessible.
5. **Text never fights the animation.** Nothing draws through a headline or body copy. Put text on calm areas or on a scrim, and keep motion around it, not across it.
6. **One meaning per material.** If molten metal means "commit", it always means commit. A language is a set of promises.

## Motion and behavior

7. **Behavior, not loops.** Flocking, springs, settling, boredom and sleep. Things react to the visitor and come back to rest.
8. **Every touch has an echo.** Hover, press, scroll and typing all leave a visible result in the world, scaled to how big the action was.
9. **One signature moment.** Give each page one thing worth recording and sharing (a whale crossing the screen, a storm you call down) and tell people how to find it.

## Respect

10. **Fast on a normal laptop.** One loop, nothing off screen running, bounded particles. The winner of round one won performance by a wide margin and that decided the title.
11. **Calm when asked.** Under reduced motion there is no loop. Show a beautiful still frame and let interactions change it.
12. **Real controls.** Real buttons and links, visible keyboard focus, Escape closes things, canvases are labeled or hidden from screen readers.
